Project Overview: Zomato Global Expansion Strategy

This project focuses on formulating a data-driven strategy for Zomato’s international expansion by identifying optimal locations for new restaurant establishments. The initiative combines data analysis, strategic planning, and actionable recommendations to guide Zomato’s growth into new global markets.

Project Components
1. Data Collection and Analysis

Data from multiple sources was compiled, cleaned, and analyzed using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focused on evaluating market trends, demographic factors, and competitive landscapes to identify promising international markets for expansion.

2. Strategic Development

Insights derived from the data analysis were summarized and presented in a PowerPoint presentation, outlining strategic approaches for Zomato’s market entry and growth. The presentation highlighted key findings, market opportunities, and recommended action plans to support informed decision-making.

3. Location-Based Recommendations

Based on analytical outcomes and strategic evaluations, the project proposed data-backed recommendations for Zomato’s new restaurant locations. These recommendations were designed to maximize market penetration and align with Zomato’s global growth objectives.

Key Highlights

Comprehensive Data Analysis:
Conducted extensive analysis using Excel to assess global market potential through research on demographics, consumer behavior, and competitors.

Strategic Planning and Presentation:
Developed and presented a strategic framework in PowerPoint, detailing actionable insights, market priorities, and expansion roadmaps.

Data-Driven Decision-Making:
Delivered targeted recommendations for new market entries grounded in quantitative and qualitative data insights.
